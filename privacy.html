Ironhold Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Ironhold handles information when you use the game and its optional Google Sign-In and cloud-save features.

2. Information We Access

If you choose to sign in with Google, Ironhold requests access to:

  • Your Google account email address, to identify your account.
  • Google Drive app data storage, to save and restore your game progress.

3. How Information Is Used

This information is used to provide Google Sign-In, associate your game progress with your account, and save or restore your game data.

4. Google Drive Data

Ironhold uses the Google Drive appDataFolder area to store its cloud-save data. This data is stored in the application's private Drive storage area and is not intended to appear as an ordinary file in your Drive.

5. Sharing

Ironhold does not sell or rent Google account information. Information accessed through Google is used to provide the features described in this policy.

6. Security

Ironhold uses Google's authentication services. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Data Deletion

Google Sign-In is optional. You can revoke Ironhold's access through your Google Account's third-party connections settings. You can contact us with questions or requests about your data.

8. Children's Privacy

Ironhold does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when Ironhold's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
